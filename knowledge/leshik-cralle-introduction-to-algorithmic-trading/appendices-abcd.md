# Appendices A–D (reference catalogs)

Source: Leshik & Cralle, *An Introduction to Algorithmic Trading* (2011)

The appendices are reference catalogs (mid-2010 snapshot), not
technical chapters — distilled here as a single index entry.

- **Appendix A — "The List" of Algo Users and Providers**: one-paragraph
  outlines of major Tier-1 algorithmic product offerings (Instinet's
  Block Peg / Cobra / Impact Minimization etc.; plus entries for other
  sell-side and ECN players). Purpose: orient the individual trader on
  the algo landscape and spark ALPHA-ALGO design ideas. Stresses the
  market's rapid evolution and systemic fragmentation (dark pools =
  anonymous institutional liquidity off the lit market).
- **Appendix B — Industry SIC-sector definitions**: the authors'
  modified sector taxonomy used for cohort selection.
- **Appendix C — Stock Watchlist**: the trading watchlist with basic
  outline info and company descriptions (the cohort source from ch. 19).
- **Appendix D — Stock Details Snapshot**: per-stock detail snapshots
  supporting the profiling workflow of ch. 20.

## Note

These are dated reference data (2010); treat as historical context, not
current. No distilled technique beyond the ch. 4 / ch. 19 / ch. 20
summaries already captured.

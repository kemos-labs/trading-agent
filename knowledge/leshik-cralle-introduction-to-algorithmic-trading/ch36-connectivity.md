# Chapter 36 — Connectivity

Source: Leshik & Cralle, *An Introduction to Algorithmic Trading* (2011)

Often neglected by individual traders, but a key to success.

- Bandwidth depends on local availability; do local research; tell
  vendors what you need the line for.
- Minimum **18 Mbps uncontended** bandwidth if possible.
- Watch for the **contention trap** — shared connectivity where more
  users = less bandwidth. Usually not a problem, but confirm with the
  provider.
- Use speed-check programs (downloadable from the net) to verify
  you're getting what you pay for.

## Key takeaway

Connectivity is infrastructure, not a detail — verify uncontended
bandwidth and actual delivered speed.

# Chapter 7 — A Perspective View from a Tier 1 Company (UBS)

Source: Leshik & Cralle, *An Introduction to Algorithmic Trading* (2011)

Guest essay by Owain Self, Executive Director, European Algorithmic
Trading at UBS (© UBS 2006).

## Building & maintaining an algo tool

- The initial "any algo capability" frenzy gave way to circumspection:
  cheap, less effective systems were found out. Clients now demand
  performance, flexibility, reliability — requiring tens of millions in
  investment and 100+ worldwide staff; limits the credible market to
  ~5–6 global broker-dealers.
- **Expertise**: balance three groups — traders, quantitative analysts,
  technology developers. No single "all-rounder" exists; over-investing
  in technology without traders/quants is unwise. QAs grow in importance
  as models/risk get sophisticated, but the best system is not the most
  mathematically exotic one — it must be usable by the average trader.
  Everyone participates in decisions.
- **Technology**: continuous evolution (UBS on its 3rd generation since
  the late 1990s). Latest generation only; avoid legacy. Development is
  incremental — find existing systems' limitations and improve ("moving
  sideways to go forward").
- **Feedback**: client feedback matters but must be contextualized
  (expectation/education levels); partnership > one-dimensional
  vendor-client; don't wait for feedback — anticipate clients and stay a
  step ahead (internal trading teams + dev team as feedback sources).
- **Other factors**: full-service ambitions, human support at all times,
  integration with back/middle office, global reach with *regional*
  expertise per market (not one monolithic product), regulatory
  awareness (MiFID in Europe, Reg NMS in the US — multi-venue access).
  Speed matters but DMA matters more; exchange membership is an edge;
  anonymity and information-leakage control while maximizing internal
  crossing. Constant, never-ending development.

## Key takeaways

1. Credible algo provision is a scale game (5–6 global players): capital
   + tripartite talent + continuous evolution.
2. For the individual trader, the analog is: understand your tool's
   components, parameters, strengths, and limitations — that confidence
   removes emotional burden (ch. 8 elaborates).

# Chapter 10 — The Future: Where Do We Go from Here?

Source: Leshik & Cralle, *An Introduction to Algorithmic Trading* (2011)

## Forecast (written 2010–11)

- **Pan-algorithmic** future: sell-side brokerages become "software
  houses" competing for buy-side business; buy side develops its own
  proprietary algos and desks — the disintermediation scenario. Sell
  side responds by aggressively algorithmizing order handling to
  preserve the relationship.
- Human traders cannot match the exponential growth in volume,
  complexity, and information (gigabytes → terabytes → petabytes);
  Tier-1 trader headcount shrinks.
- **AI/neural networks/genetic algorithms** will reach critical mass:
  "intelligent" algos making their own decisions; predictive algos with
  a reasonable hit rate and stretching predictive horizons. A "shoal of
  rapacious ALPHA ALGOS" is expected in the algo space; the authors
  position this book as the vanguard for the individual trader.

## Key takeaways

1. The strategic direction: automation migrating to the buy side and
   individuals; competitive edge shifts from execution to prediction
   and AI-assisted signal generation.
2. (Historical note: the book predates the modern AI wave; treat the
   specifics as context, not prescription.)

- Fixed hypotheses (no OOS search):
  - `dual_sma_9_45` — long when SMA(9) > SMA(45), flat otherwise.
  - `donchian_50_20` — long/flat breakout: enter above prior 50-bar high, exit below prior 20-bar low (channels are prior-bar extrema; position persists; executed next bar).
  - `vol_mom_252_60_10pct` — long when 252-day momentum > 0, scaled to 10% annual vol on a 60-day window, capped at 1.5×.
- Baseline: equal-weight buy-and-hold (daily mean of asset simple returns, same cost model).
- PASS (all): OOS total return > 0, OOS Sharpe > buy-and-hold Sharpe, OOS max DD shallower than buy-and-hold, and positive OOS return on ≥ 2/3 assets. Otherwise REJECT.

## Interpretation

**Candidate(s) for further validation:** dual_sma_9_45, vol_mom_252_60_10pct. A PASS under this narrow three-ETF, single-holdout protocol is *not* approval to trade — it only means the hypothesis survived a minimal real-data screen and deserves broader walk-forward, cross-asset, and transaction-cost sensitivity checks before any paper trading.

Common failure modes to check before any follow-up: insufficient diversification (N=3), regime dependence (2019–2024 was mostly a trending bull market with a 2020/2022 drawdown), and the equal-weight assumption itself. The FMZ catalog remains useful only as a source of *other* fixed hypotheses to test under the same protocol, not as evidence of profitability.
